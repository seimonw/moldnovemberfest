Mali Sigsworth

Saturday afternoon will feature the beautiful voice of Mali Sigsworth, performing a mix of Country, Folk and Soul. With her heartfelt energy and stripped-back acoustic style, Mali’s performance promises to provide the perfect backdrop to a relaxed afternoon enjoying some excellent ales and ciders.
